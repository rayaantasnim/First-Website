Story Writing: A Greedy Farmer

Once, a humble farmer and his greedy wife lived in a village.

One day, the farmer found a wounded goose. He nursed it back to health, and to his astonishment, it laid a golden egg.

The farmer sold the egg and shared the modest wealth with his neighbors. But his wife grew impatient. “Why wait for one egg a day? Slice open the goose and take all the gold at once!” she demanded.

The farmer refused, fearing it would destroy their luck. But his wife’s greed overpowered him.

Reluctantly, he cut open the goose—only to find nothing inside. The magic was gone, and so was their fortune. The wife wailed in regret, while the farmer sighed, “Greed blinds wisdom.”

They returned to poverty, their chance at prosperity lost forever.
