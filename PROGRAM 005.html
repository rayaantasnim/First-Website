Current Hobby:

  1. Gardening: I turn my garden into a small paradise through love and patience. First, I prepare the soil, enriching it with organic compost to make it fertile and alive. Then, I carefully arrange colorful flower saplings and plants, ensuring something blooms in every season. Regular watering, timely fertilizing, and diligent weeding keep my plants vibrant and thriving. I add charm with stone pathways, decorative pebbles, and soft garden lights to enhance its beauty. Watching each leaf and flower grow reminds me that this little connection with nature is my greatest joy.

  2. Reading: Losing myself in the pages of a book during my free time is my ultimate joy. I step into new worlds, meet fascinating characters, and wander through realms of history or imagination. My heart races during thrilling moments, tears well up at poignant scenes, and laughter bursts out at witty remarks. With each turned page, I discover fresh knowledge or perspectives that broaden my mind. I build a silent kinship with the characters, sharing their journeys. Even in solitude, a book becomes a faithful companion, offering profound, soulful conversations. This quiet adventure truly nourishes my spirit and fills me with happiness.

  3. Foreign Currency Collecting: Whenever I travel or connect with friends abroad, I collect currency as tangible pieces of the world. My collection includes the intricate designs of the Euro, the iconic US Dollar, Japan’s minimalist Yen, Saudi Arabia’s regal Riyal, Bangladeshi Taka, the UAE’s geometric Dirham, and Sweden’s artful Krona. Each coin and note tells a story – of a nation’s history, culture, and identity. Holding them feels like owning fragments of distant lands. The thrill of discovering a new currency, studying its symbols, and preserving its legacy turns every addition into a mini treasure hunt. This hobby isn’t just about accumulation; it’s my passport to global appreciation.

  4. Learning Interesting Science: In my leisure time, I dive into 'Biggan Chinta,' a science monthly magazine that fuels my curiosity. It unravels the mysteries of the human brain’s neural networks, demystifies the technology behind the Rooppur Nuclear Power Plant, and explains mind-bending concepts like time dilation from relativity. I also discover fascinating animal adaptations – from tigers to dolphins. Each article presents complex ideas with clarity, making advanced science accessible. Whether exploring cosmic wonders or Earth’s biodiversity, this magazine turns every page into an adventure. It keeps my mind ignited and teaches me to see the world through a lens of awe and inquiry.
